Ringfall Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Ringfall ("the game") is made by Changyoon Lee ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. The game has no sign-in.

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, items and settings) is saved only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking)
  • your IP address and approximate location
  • device type, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Ads in the game are optional except for an occasional short ad between levels. Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one.

Your choices

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in the shop.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your information with Ad privacy choices in the shop.
  • iPhone: you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).

If you decline personalized ads, you may still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your information. Apart from the advertising described above, the game shares no information with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
